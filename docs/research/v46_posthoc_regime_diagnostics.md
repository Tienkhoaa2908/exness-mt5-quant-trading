# V46 post-hoc regime diagnostics (research queue only)

Date: 2026-08-22

This note records diagnostics discovered after accepted V45 evidence. None of these observations are part of the V46 primary and none may be promoted from the V45 sample.

## Market context

2022 and 2024 must not be treated as the same regime. 2022 combined Russia-Ukraine safe-haven demand with high inflation, rapid rate increases and a strong US dollar; standing aside can be a valid response to that conflicting-force transition. 2024, by contrast, was a strongly rising gold year with many new all-time highs, so a large strategy loss in 2024 remains evidence of a routing/timing weakness.

No calendar event or war-date label should be introduced into strategy logic. Any crisis protection must be inferred from causal market or ensemble state.

## Accepted V45 trade-level observations

HL10p05 after warm-up, approximate SumR by direction:

- 2022: LONG -22.22R, SHORT -8.81R;
- 2023: LONG +11.06R, SHORT +7.44R;
- 2024: LONG -23.92R, SHORT -4.36R;
- 2025: LONG +52.24R, SHORT +20.51R;
- 2026 Jan-Jul: LONG +37.03R, SHORT +43.25R.

Therefore 2024 is not explained simply by shorting a rising market; long timing also failed.

## Research-only feature observations

Using V45 trade entries after seeing the result:

- an ADX upper-bound observation (`ADX <= 30`) materially improved 2024 replay and retained substantial 2025-2026 R;
- direction/DI alignment (`LONG: +DI > -DI`, `SHORT: -DI > +DI`) improved aggregate trade-R, though it did not fully solve 2022/2024;
- a simple ATR-ratio cap did not adequately solve the weak years.

These are post-hoc findings. They are not valid V46 promotion evidence and must not be inserted into the V46 breadth4 primary. If breadth4 does not meet the preregistered V46 gate, a later V47 may preregister a small number of causal price-state gates and test them under a new protocol.

## Engineering interpretation

The objective is not to require positive PnL in every crisis year. The objective is to detect when the system's edge is absent and reduce new risk fast enough to preserve capital. A crisis/transition period may legitimately be flat or mildly negative; a catastrophic drawdown is not acceptable.
